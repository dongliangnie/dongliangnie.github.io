Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) can learn world models directly in latent space, but insufficient constraints lead to representation collapse. Sub-JEPA relaxes the global isotropic Gaussian prior used by LeWorldModel by applying Gaussian regularization across multiple random subspaces, suppressing collapse while retaining the geometric richness of the representation.
